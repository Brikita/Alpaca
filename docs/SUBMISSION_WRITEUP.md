# VolGuard AI

**An autonomous, risk-governed options desk that knows when not to trade.**

VolGuard AI was built for a simple problem: an automated trading system should not be judged only by how often it places an order. It should be judged by whether every order is supported by fresh evidence, sized before entry, reconciled after execution, and managed by rules that cannot be improvised away under pressure.

VolGuard turns an Alpaca paper account into a transparent options workflow. It scans SPY, QQQ, IWM, and GLD; compares a realized-volatility model with the live at-the-money options price; and runs six signal-quality checks covering the market session, price history, paired quotes, liquidity, quote freshness, and edge. A failed check produces an explicit abstention instead of a fabricated opportunity.

When a signal survives, the position constructor chooses exact contracts and prices them conservatively—buys at the ask and sells at the bid. It supports defined-risk bull-call and bear-put verticals, long straddles, and a wing optimizer that can convert an oversized two-sided volatility thesis into a capped reverse iron butterfly. The proposal then goes to a four-role specialist council: Regime tests directional alignment, Volatility tests edge and execution quality, Catalyst abstains until a verified event feed exists, and Red Team can veto malformed, uncovered, same-day, unprofitable, or over-budget structures. The council provides evidence, but it cannot override the deterministic governor.

The final authority is a thirteen-gate risk policy. It requires paper mode, defined loss, covered short legs, no same-day expiry, no more than $500 maximum loss per strategy, no more than $1,000 combined open risk, at most two strategy lifecycles, daily drawdown below $1,500, competition drawdown below $4,000, correlation capacity, bid-ask spreads at or below 12%, quotes no older than 60 seconds, and at least two specialist approvals with a red-team non-veto and 65% confidence. VolGuard also blocks stacking two positions on the same underlying.

Execution uses the official Alpaca CLI and Alpaca Trading API. Every entry is first validated as one atomic multi-leg dry run. Paper submission requires an explicit one-process unlock, an idempotent client order ID, fresh evidence, and the exact legs approved by the governor. Live routing is prohibited. Broker positions are then matched back to the recorded strategy before the system is allowed to manage or close it.

The first governed paper execution demonstrates the complete path. During the September 1 session, SPY and QQQ abstained while IWM and GLD produced bearish candidates. VolGuard ranked GLD first, constructed the September 4 GLD 398/391 bear-put spread, and calculated a conservative $1.82 debit limit, $182 maximum loss, and $518 theoretical maximum expiration profit. All six signal checks, the specialist council, and all thirteen portfolio gates cleared. Alpaca accepted the atomic paper order and filled it at an average $1.79 debit. Later five-minute monitor cycles reconciled both broker legs, repriced the exit conservatively, and recorded HOLD because no precommitted profit, loss, or time threshold had been reached.

The operational architecture keeps brokerage authority away from the browser. A local paper-only runner uses Alpaca credentials and publishes only schema-validated, sanitized telemetry. Cloudflare D1 stores append-only account snapshots, scans, decisions, order events, fills, and lifecycle checks. A Cloudflare Worker triggers the authenticated GitHub Actions monitor every five minutes on trading weekdays. The responsive dashboard exposes Overview, Decisions, Positions, Risk, and Journal pages with EAT, ET, and UTC display modes, while never exposing broker credentials or raw identifiers.

VolGuard's creative contribution is not another opaque buy/sell bot. It is an inspectable operating system for disciplined options trading: autonomous enough to scan, submit, reconcile, and monitor; constrained enough to abstain; and documented well enough for a human to learn from every trade and non-trade. The same product is a hackathon entry today and a tangible paper-trading practice environment for improving a real trading routine tomorrow.

**Safety:** VolGuard is an educational paper-trading system. It is not investment advice and must not be connected to real capital.
