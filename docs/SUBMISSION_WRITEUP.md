# VolGuard AI

**An autonomous, risk-governed options desk that knows when not to trade.**

VolGuard AI was built for a simple problem: an automated trading system should not be judged only by how often it places an order. It should be judged by whether every order is supported by fresh evidence, sized before entry, reconciled after execution, and managed by rules that cannot be improvised away under pressure.

VolGuard turns an Alpaca paper account into a transparent options workflow. It scans SPY, QQQ, IWM, and GLD; compares a realized-volatility model with the live at-the-money options price; and runs six signal-quality checks covering the market session, price history, paired quotes, liquidity, quote freshness, and edge. A failed check produces an explicit abstention instead of a fabricated opportunity.

When a signal survives, the position constructor chooses exact contracts and prices them conservatively—buys at the ask and sells at the bid. It supports defined-risk bull-call and bear-put verticals, long straddles, and a wing optimizer that can convert an oversized two-sided volatility thesis into a capped reverse iron butterfly. The proposal then goes to a five-role specialist council: Regime tests directional alignment, Volatility tests edge and execution quality, Catalyst requires a clear verified Alpaca news snapshot, Decision Memory compares the setup with recent D1-stored analysis, and Red Team can veto malformed, uncovered, same-day, unprofitable, or over-budget structures. The council provides evidence, but it cannot override the deterministic governor.

Decision Memory makes “learning from prior analysis” concrete without pretending that a small sample proves predictive edge. It examines at most six open-market observations from the prior 60 minutes. A current candidate needs at least two scans agreeing on symbol, strategy, and direction, and at least 60% of recent observations must agree. The agent reports confirmations, agreement, signal-strength change, and median spread. Missing, conflicting, closed-market, or first-sighting evidence fails closed, and memory can never convert an abstention into a candidate.

The final authority is a fourteen-gate risk policy. It requires paper mode, defined loss, covered short legs, no same-day expiry, no more than $500 maximum loss per strategy, at least 0.25x theoretical maximum reward relative to defined loss for capped structures, no more than $1,000 combined open risk, at most two strategy lifecycles, daily drawdown below $1,500, competition drawdown below $4,000, correlation capacity, bid-ask spreads at or below 12%, quotes no older than 60 seconds, a clear Catalyst vote, a confirmed Memory vote, a red-team non-veto, sufficient council approval, and 65% proposal confidence. VolGuard also blocks stacking two positions on the same underlying.

Execution uses the official Alpaca CLI and Alpaca Trading API. Every entry is first validated as one atomic multi-leg dry run. Paper submission requires an explicit one-process unlock, an idempotent client order ID, fresh evidence, and the exact legs approved by the governor. Live routing is prohibited. Broker positions are then matched back to the recorded strategy before the system is allowed to manage or close it.

The first governed paper execution demonstrates the original complete execution path. During the September 1 session, SPY and QQQ abstained while IWM and GLD produced bearish candidates. VolGuard ranked GLD first, constructed the September 4 GLD 398/391 bear-put spread, and calculated a conservative $1.82 debit limit, $182 maximum loss, and $518 theoretical maximum expiration profit. All six signal checks, the then-current four-role council, and all thirteen then-current portfolio gates cleared. Alpaca accepted the atomic paper order and filled it at an average $1.79 debit. The lifecycle monitor later closed the spread and recorded a $165 realized loss. Decision Memory was added afterward, so the submission does not retroactively attribute memory approval to that historical fill.

The September 3 session proved the stricter memory-enabled path. GLD was confirmed in six of six open-market scans over 50 minutes, all five specialists approved, and the 407/412/417 long iron butterfly passed the then-current thirteen gates. Alpaca accepted the atomic order at a $4.76 debit limit and filled it at $4.55; the next five-minute cycle matched all four broker legs and recorded a deterministic HOLD. That live result also exposed a genuine design flaw: the conservative proposal risked $476 for only $24 of theoretical maximum profit. Instead of hiding the evidence, the project used it to add a fourteenth payoff-quality gate and matching Red Team veto. Future capped positions must now offer at least 0.25x theoretical maximum reward relative to defined loss, and weak wing combinations are rejected during construction.

The operational architecture keeps brokerage authority away from the browser. A paper-only runner uses Alpaca credentials and publishes only schema-validated, sanitized telemetry. Cloudflare D1 stores append-only account snapshots, scans, decisions, order events, fills, lifecycle checks, and daily replay evidence; Decision Memory derives its verdict from that same immutable scan trail. A Cloudflare Worker triggers one authenticated GitHub Actions cycle every five minutes on trading weekdays: every run monitors exits, while every other run also refreshes the account, scans for a new opportunity, and attempts one fully governed paper entry. A strongly consistent Durable Object emergency control can stop future dispatches, and GitHub Issues surface failures, safety holds, and accepted paper trades. The responsive dashboard exposes Overview, Decisions, Positions, Risk, Journal, and Performance pages with EAT, ET, and UTC display modes, while never exposing broker credentials or raw identifiers.

VolGuard's creative contribution is not another opaque buy/sell bot. It is an inspectable operating system for disciplined options trading: autonomous enough to scan, submit, reconcile, and monitor; constrained enough to abstain; and documented well enough for a human to learn from every trade and non-trade. The same product is a hackathon entry today and a tangible paper-trading practice environment for improving a real trading routine tomorrow.

**Safety:** VolGuard is an educational paper-trading system. It is not investment advice and must not be connected to real capital.
